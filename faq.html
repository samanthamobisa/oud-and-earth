Frequently Asked Questions

Ordering & Shipping

Orders within Nairobi typically arrive within 1–2 business days. Deliveries to other parts of Kenya take 3–5 business days. We currently do not ship internationally, but this is something we're working on.

Browse our Shop page, choose your fragrance, and use the Contact form to send an enquiry with the product name and your delivery details. We'll confirm availability and payment within a few hours.

We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer, and major debit/credit cards. Payment details are shared once your order is confirmed.

Returns & Refunds

For hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns on opened or used bottles. If your bottle arrives sealed and unused, you may return it within 7 days for a full refund or exchange.

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the damage and we'll send a replacement at no extra cost.

Choosing a Fragrance

With 3–4 sprays per wear, a 50ml bottle typically lasts 3–5 months of regular use. Our fragrances are concentrated, so a little goes a long way.

We label scents Men, Women, or Unisex as a general guide based on traditional notes, but fragrance is personal — we encourage you to wear whatever makes you feel confident, regardless of category.
