Our Story

Born From the Ground Up

How We Started

A Workshop, Not a Factory

Oud & Earth began in 2021 in a small workshop in Nairobi, founded on one belief: the best fragrances don't mask the wearer, they reveal them. Our founder, a chemistry graduate with a fascination for traditional perfumery, grew frustrated with synthetic, mass-produced scents that smelled identical to a thousand others.

So she started blending her own — sourcing raw oud chips, Somali frankincense, and wild vetiver directly from growers across East Africa and the Arabian Peninsula. What started as gifts for friends became a waiting list, then a small batch run, then Oud & Earth.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🌍

Sourced, Not Synthesized

We work directly with growers and distillers, never with synthetic substitutes. If it can't be traced to a farm or forest, it doesn't go in the bottle.

✋

Hand-Blended

Every batch is mixed and aged by hand in our Nairobi workshop. No automated production lines — just patience and a trained nose.

🌱

Sustainably Harvested

We partner only with growers who replant what they harvest, protecting the oud forests and resin trees for the next generation.

Our Process

From Raw Material to Bottle

Each fragrance takes between 6 and 14 weeks to complete. Raw materials are inspected, distilled, and macerated before blending begins. Once mixed, every batch rests for a minimum of 30 days, allowing the notes to settle and meld before it's ever bottled.

We test every batch by wear, not just by smell — applying it to skin and tracking how it evolves over an 8-hour day before approving a release.
